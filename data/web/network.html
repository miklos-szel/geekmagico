1. Set your city first

Used for weather and, in automatic mode, for the timezone.

Location example: for the capital city of Korea, enter Seoul. A numeric OpenWeatherMap city ID also works.

2. Set internet connection

Select a WiFi network with a strong signal. 2.4GHz only — the ESP8266 has no 5GHz radio.

SSIDSignal

After connecting successfully the "GeekMagicO" hotspot is turned off and the new IP address is shown on the screen. Connect your phone or computer to the same WiFi and open that address to keep changing settings.
